# 認識世界 🌏

【大自然】🌱
// 摸得到、看得到的植物和動物：去公園、去動物園的時候拿出來對照

// 從種子到大樹：Plant a seed. → The sprout grows up. → The tree is tall.

卡: leaf | 葉子 | 🍃 | The leaf is green. | The leaf falls down. | Look at the big leaf.

卡: tree | 樹 | 🌳 | The tree is tall. | Birds live in the tree. | Let's sit under the tree.
卡: grass | 草 | 🌿 | The grass is green. | The cow eats grass. | Let's run on the grass.
卡: sunflower | 向日葵 | 🌻 | The sunflower is yellow. | The sunflower looks at the sun. | The sunflower is very tall.
卡: rose | 玫瑰 | 🌹 | The rose is red. | The rose smells sweet. | Careful! The rose has thorns.

【我自己】🧒
// 我的身體會感覺、我的心情會變：認識自己，也學會照顧自己

字卡本: 五感 | Five Senses
階段: 小班～中班
// 正面是眼睛、耳朵，問「What can you do with it?」（中文：可以用它做什麼？），答案是 see、hear。問的時候不加 the
卡: see | 看 | 👀 | I see with my eyes. | Look! What do you see? | I can see a rainbow.
卡: hear | 聽 | 👂 | I hear with my ears. | Shh! What do you hear? | I can hear a bird.
卡: smell | 聞 | 👃 | I smell with my nose. | Sniff, sniff! What do you smell? | The flower smells good.
卡: taste | 嚐 | 👅 | I taste with my tongue. | Yum! What do you taste? | The lemon tastes sour.
卡: touch | 摸 | ✋ | I touch with my hands. | Touch it. Is it soft? | The teddy bear feels soft.

階段: 小班～中班

卡: hug | 抱一抱 | 🤗 | Can I have a hug? | A big hug makes me feel better. | Hug your teddy bear.
卡: talk about it | 說出來 | 🗣️ | Tell me how you feel. | I feel angry. Can you help me? | Use your words.
卡: ask for help | 找大人幫忙 | 🙋 | Ask a grown-up for help. | Can you help me, please? | It's okay to ask for help.
卡: take a break | 休息一下 | 😌 | Take a break. | Sit in a quiet place. | Rest for a little while.
